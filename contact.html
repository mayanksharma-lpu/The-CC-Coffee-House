Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from our guests.

Do I need prior booking for the Party Hall?
Yes, we recommend reserving the Party Hall at least 48 hours in advance to customize neon lights, seating arrangements, music setup, and specialized catering menus. Contact us via the form above or call directly.
What are your delivery hours and coverage area?
Our express local delivery operates daily from 8:30 AM to 10:30 PM within a 10 km radius of the café. Piping fresh coffee is delivered in insulated thermal containers, guaranteed hot or iced as ordered!
Are vegan and dairy-free milk options available?
Absolutely! We offer Almond Milk, Oat Milk, and Soy Milk substitutes for all our hot and cold espresso drinks, matcha lattes, and frappes. Just mention your preference when ordering.
Do you offer high-speed WiFi for remote work?
Yes! Our Work & Study Nook features complimentary 300 Mbps high-speed WiFi along with charging ports and USB hubs at every booth. Perfect for focused work sessions with great coffee by your side.
Can I order custom catering for corporate events?
Yes! We offer premium corporate catering packages including coffee stations, finger food, pastry platters, and customized menus. Fill out the contact form above with "Catering & Bulk Order" and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
